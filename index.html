Mission

To provide students and community members with easy, fair, and organized access to sports equipment that supports physical activity, teamwork, and wellness.

Vision

To build a borrowing system where every student can easily access sports equipment—without hassle, confusion, or delay—so they can stay active, healthy, and engaged in campus life.

Core Values

Accessibility, Responsibility, Fairness, Teamwork, and Service.

Welcome to the Sports Equipment Borrowing System

This system makes it easy for students and community members to borrow sports equipment without hassle. Whether you're preparing for PE class, joining a tournament, or simply staying active, our goal is to provide a simple, fair, and organized way to access the gear you need. No more confusion, no more delays—just smooth borrowing and better play.
